0303 / 06

Agentic Sprint: Validate Your AI Idea in Just 10 Days.

01.

Frame

DAYS 1–2

  • Pick the one use case with real business value
  • Define success metrics the board will recognise
  • Confirm data access and technical feasibility

We don’t build until case and the data are real.

02.

Build

DAYS 3–8

  • A working agent on your data in your environment
  • Reasoning, tool use and memory, not a demo
  • Guardrails a human-in-the-loop from day one

A prototype you can actually pressure-test.

03.

Prove

DAYS 9–10

  • Measured against the KPIs you set on day one
  • A costed roadmap from prototype to production
  • A clear build, buy or partner decision

You leave with a decision, not a maybe.

Don’t debate it for months. Prove it in 10 days.

Why Choose Q For AI Projects?

01
Large Local AI Models

All AI development and testing is performed within our infrastructure. Our software development process is enhanced by large local AI models that support specification, code generation, testing, and review — enabling our teams to focus on quality, validation, and delivering exceptional products.

02
AI Academy

To continuously strengthen our capabilities, we run an internal AI Academy. Over 100 Q’s experts have already completed the program, specializing in modern AI agent development, with top performers earning AWS AI certifications.

03
Private Infrastructure: Nvidia H200 GPU Cluster

We invested in our own AI infrastructure, powered by a dedicated NVIDIA H200 GPU cluster. This allows us to train, fine-tune, and deploy AI models entirely on our own hardware — ensuring speed, independence, and maximum data security.
