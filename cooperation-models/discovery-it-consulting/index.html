FAQ

01

What is a dedicated software development team?

A dedicated software development team is a team of selected programmers who will be working exclusively on your software solution. They are an outsourced team, either nearshore or offshore.

02

How is a dedicated team managed?

A team of dedicated software developers can be managed either by your company or by the project manager from the company that you hired the team from.

03

What are the most trusted countries where expert, dedicated development teams can be hired?

Eastern European countries are among the top destinations for hiring dedicated software teams. You can see that If you look at platforms like Clutch.

We are based in Croatia, the youngest member of the EU, in the Central European time zone (Berlin, Paris). Your dedicated developers speak fluent English, are seasoned IT professionals who can be hired at the same rate as a junior-mid developer in London, New York or Zurich.

04

What types of projects are best suited for a dedicated agile team?

This model is ideal for B2C & B2B products requiring continuous updates, such as SaaS, enterprise software, e-commerce, and large platforms, particularly for projects lasting 6+ months.

05

What kind of support can I expect after project completion?

We offer ongoing support, updates, and maintenance to ensure your software solution operates effectively, without interruption. Additionally, we provide expert consultations and advice to keep you up-to-date with the latest technology trends. Our goal is to build long-term partnerships with our clients, where we go beyond just development. We actively work with you to align our solutions with your evolving business needs, helping you achieve sustained growth and success.
